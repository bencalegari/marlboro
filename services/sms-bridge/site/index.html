What this is

Marlboro Media is a private, self-hosted media server run for one household and a small number of friends. Members text the name of a movie or TV series to the program number and receive one message back when it is available to watch.

This is not a commercial service. Nothing is sold, and no marketing, promotional, or advertising messages are ever sent.

How it works

1. A member texts a title
Every conversation is started by the member. The service never messages first.
2. The service replies with matches
A short numbered list. The member replies with a number to choose one.
3. One notification when it is ready
Sent only for the title that member personally requested.

Who receives messages

Only people who asked to be included and gave their phone number directly to the operator. Numbers are added by hand to a private allowlist. A number that is not on that allowlist receives no messages of any kind. There is no web signup, and numbers are never purchased, rented, imported, or obtained from any third party.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. Members can also ask the operator to remove their number, which deletes it from the allowlist.
