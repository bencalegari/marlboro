Program description

Marlboro Media is a private media server run for one household and a small number of friends. Members text the name of a movie or TV series to the program number (+1 (812) 616-4925) and receive a reply with matching titles, plus one notification when the title they requested becomes available to watch. Messages are account notifications only. This is not a commercial service and no marketing messages are sent.

How you join

Membership is by direct arrangement with the operator. You asked to be included and gave your number in person, and you start every conversation yourself by texting a title to the program number. The service never messages you first.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Messages are sent only in response to your own requests, so the volume is determined entirely by you. In practice this is a few messages per week across all members.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. The program itself is free; your mobile carrier's standard messaging and data charges apply to messages you send and receive.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will be removed from the sending list and will receive no further messages. You may also ask the operator directly to delete your number.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for a description of the program and how to opt out, or email lap.rapper_3o@icloud.com.

Supported carriers and delivery

Delivery is subject to your carrier's network and is not guaranteed. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Eligibility

You must be the owner or authorised user of the mobile number you send from, and at least 13 years old.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: your number is used only to deliver these messages, and no mobile information or opt-in data is sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
