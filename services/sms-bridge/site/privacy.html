What we collect

Only your mobile phone number, and the text of the messages you send to the program number so that a request can be acted on. No other personal information is collected. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising identifiers.

How your number is used

Your number is used for exactly one purpose: delivering the notifications described in the Terms & Conditions — a reply to a request you sent, and one message when the title you requested becomes available.

We do not share it

No mobile information, including phone numbers and opt-in data, is sold, rented, shared, or otherwise disclosed to any third party. It is not shared with affiliates or partners, and it is never used for marketing or lead generation.

Messages are transmitted through Twilio, our messaging provider, solely to deliver them to your carrier. Twilio processes them on our behalf and for no other purpose.

Where it is stored

Phone numbers are stored on hardware operated by us, not with any external service. The allowlist is maintained privately and is not published.

How long it is kept

Your number is kept only while you are a member of the program. It is deleted on request, or when you opt out.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages and be removed from the sending list. Reply HELP for help. You may also email us to have your number deleted.

Children

This service is not directed to children and no one under 13 is knowingly included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
